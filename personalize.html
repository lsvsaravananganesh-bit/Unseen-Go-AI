A planner built around preferences

Instead of giving everyone the same list, UnseenGo uses the traveler's inputs to prioritize destinations and balance the journey.

  • Interests such as heritage, nature, food and culture.
  • Trip duration and available time.
  • Slow, balanced or fast travel pace.
  • Budget style and practical route constraints.
