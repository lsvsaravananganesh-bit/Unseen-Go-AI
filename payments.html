Payment Processing Guidelines

1. Zero Hidden Platform Markups

When you book through verified partner links or direct host integrations on UnseenGo, the final amount displayed at checkout reflects the total cost, inclusive of applicable GST (Goods and Services Tax) and statutory service fees. We do not apply undisclosed surge markups at final payment.

2. Supported Payment Methods

  • UPI (Unified Payments Interface): Instant scan-and-pay QR codes and VPA payment requests via BHIM, PhonePe, GPay, and Cred.
  • Debit & Credit Cards: Domestic and international RuPay, Visa, MasterCard, and American Express cards with mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (OTP/3D Secure).
  • Direct NetBanking: Support for 50+ scheduled commercial banks across India.
  • Direct Host Cash-on-Arrival: Select verified rural homestays accept direct cash payments upon check-in to accommodate remote village banking realities.

3. Cancellations & Refund Timelines

All refund requests are processed according to our transparent tier schedule. Approved refunds are credited back to the original source payment instrument within 5 to 7 business days. For complete details, consult our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

4. Payment Dispute Support

Encountered an erroneous deduction or dual charge? Our financial operations desk is on standby. Send your transaction reference ID, payment timestamp, and booking number to billing@unseengo.ai, and our desk will respond within 4 hours.
