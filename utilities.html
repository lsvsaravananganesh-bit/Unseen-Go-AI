✦ Cultural Protocols & Remote Terrain Protocols

🛕 Temple Dress & Footwear Etiquette

Always remove footwear before entering sanctums. Wear modest clothing covering shoulders and knees. In traditional shrines of South India (like Tirupati or Guruvayur), male devotees may be required to wear dhotis without shirts. Circumambulate the sanctum clockwise (Pradakshina).

🏔️ Altitude Sickness (AMS) Protocol

For destinations above 8,000 ft (Leh, Spiti, Kedarnath), spend your first 24–36 hours completely resting without heavy physical exertion. Drink at least 3–4 litres of warm water/fluids daily. Avoid alcohol. Carry Diamox if prescribed by a physician.

📜 Inner Line Permits (ILP)

Certain sensitive border areas require prior online permit clearances: Ladakh (Nubra, Pangong, Tso Moriri), Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Mizoram, and parts of Sikkim. Keep at least 4 physical passport-size photos and ID photocopies handy.
