Essential Policy Inclusions for Exploring India

When choosing a travel insurance policy for domestic or international travel across India, verify that these four items are included in your schedule:

  • Emergency Medical Expense (Minimum $50,000 / ₹25 Lakhs): India has world-class private hospitals in major tier-1 and tier-2 hubs, but upfront deposits are required without direct cashless network tie-ups.
  • Emergency Medical Repatriation: Essential for foreign travelers who may need medical transport back to their home country.
  • Baggage & Tech Gear Loss: Protection for cameras, lenses, smartphones, and laptops against loss during bus or sleeper train transits.
  • Adventure Activity Rider: Standard travel insurance often excludes trekking above 2,500m (e.g. Himalayas) or bouldering unless a specific sports rider is added.
"Always save your policy certificate offline on your smartphone and carry a laminated copy of your policy number and 24/7 global emergency telephone line."

What to Do During a Medical Emergency in India

  1. Dial 112 (National Emergency Service) or 108 (Free State Ambulance).
  2. Call your insurer’s 24-hour emergency assistance hotline immediately before major procedures are undertaken.
  3. Retain all itemized hospital pharmacy receipts, medical reports, doctor prescriptions, and discharge summaries in original format.
  4. If filing a claim for theft or lost luggage, register a First Information Report (FIR) at the nearest local police station within 24 hours.
