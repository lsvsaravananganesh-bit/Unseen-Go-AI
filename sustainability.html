✦ Leave No Trace in India

Travel lightly. Leave beauty behind.

Exploring offbeat canyons, rainforest reserves, and centuries-old cave temples comes with profound responsibility. Here is our ethical charter for every UnseenGo traveler.

🚯

Zero Plastic Trail Code

Canyons like Gandikota and waterfalls like Talakona lack municipal waste infrastructure. Carry refillable insulated water bottles and pack all waste back to city centers.

🛕

Monument Sanctity

Ancient sandstone temples are living heritage. Never touch ancient frescoes, never carve graffiti on stone, and respect footwear boundaries in sanctums.

🪙

Direct Village Economies

Buy seasonal fruits from local road farmers, patronize family-run homestays, and hire certified local guides so tourism directly funds community wellbeing.
