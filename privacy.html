✦ Transparent Data Architecture

Your privacy, respected on every trail.

UnseenGo AI is founded on the principle that exploration should never cost your digital autonomy. Here is an honest, plain-English breakdown of what data we handle and how we safeguard it.

Last updated: October 2026 · Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act

1. Our Privacy Pledge

When you search for offbeat canyons in Kadapa or temple caves in Kurnool, we believe your travel curiosities belong to you. We do not build commercial surveillance profiles, sell browsing data to data brokers, or target invasive cross-site advertising.

2. Information We Process

We process the minimum necessary data to provide our AI-driven tourism services:

  • Travel Preferences & Itineraries: Chosen themes (e.g. Heritage, Nature, Spiritual, Budget), starting cities, duration, and pace that you submit to generate AI travel plans.
  • Account Credentials: If you register an account, we store your email and hashed password (via secure authentication) to sync your saved destinations across devices.
  • Location Data: Only processed client-side when you explicitly click "Use My Location" in the transport or route planning tool. We do not track background GPS coordinates.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized HTTP logs (user-agent, response codes, performance latencies) to maintain platform uptime and mitigate abuse.
"We never monetize traveler personal data. Our business model is based on transparent software tools and direct partnership with verified cultural heritage stays."

3. How We Use Travel Data

We use information strictly for:

  • Generating personalized, explainable hidden-gem recommendations.
  • Computing realistic travel times, terrain cautions, and daylight windows.
  • Remembering your saved bucket-list destinations inside your Travel Vault.
  • Preventing malicious bot scraping and securing community review submissions.

4. Client-Side & Local Storage

To respect your privacy, your theme preferences (Dark/Light mode), current city hub choice, and offline itinerary drafts are saved locally inside your device’s browser storage (localStorage). You can purge this at any time through your browser settings or your account profile.

5. Your Rights & Data Control

Under the DPDP Act and global data standards, you possess full control over your information:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can view all saved places and trip plans directly from your Traveler Profile.
  • Right to Erase: You can delete your account and all associated itineraries with a single click in your profile settings.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can explore UnseenGo AI anonymously as a guest without creating an account.

6. Contact Our Grievance Officer

If you have any questions, complaints, or requests concerning personal information, please reach out to our dedicated privacy desk at privacy@unseengo.ai or through our Support Center.
