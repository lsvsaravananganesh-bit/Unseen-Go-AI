Official e-Visa Application Checklist

Citizens of over 160 eligible countries can apply for the Indian e-Visa entirely online through the official Government of India portal:

  • Passport Validity: Your machine-readable passport must have at least 6 months of remaining validity from your arrival date and two blank pages for immigration stamps.
  • Digital Photograph: Recent front-facing square photograph with a clear white background (JPEG format, 10 KB to 1 MB).
  • Passport Scan: Clear color scan of your passport's biographical page containing your photo and personal details (PDF format, 10 KB to 300 KB).
  • Return / Onward Ticket: Proof of onward journey out of India.
⚠️ Beware of Fake Visa Intermediary Websites

Always submit your application directly on the official Government of India portal: indianvisaonline.gov.in. Avoid third-party commercial portals that charge inflated processing surcharges.

Protected & Restricted Area Permits (PAP / RAP)

While 95% of UnseenGo heritage destinations (across Andhra, Karnataka, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Maharashtra, Goa) are open to all tourists without special permits:

  • Certain border sectors in Ladakh (Pangong Tso, Nubra Valley), Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh, and the Andaman Islands require an additional Inner Line Permit (ILP) or Protected Area Permit (PAP).
  • These can be easily obtained through registered local tour operators or district magistrate offices upon arrival in the respective state capital.

Airport Entry Gateways

e-Visa holders can enter India through 29 designated international airports (including New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, and Kochi) and 5 designated seaports.
