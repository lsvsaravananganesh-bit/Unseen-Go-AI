Cookie & Local Storage Policy

We use minimal cookies and local browser storage to keep your theme intact, remember your active Indian destination hub, and power fast offline travel tools.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files sent to your browser by websites you visit. In modern web applications, browsers also provide localStorage, which stores key-value pairs locally on your device without transmitting data over the internet with every request.

3. Specific Storage Keys in Use

For complete technical transparency, here are the exact keys stored by UnseenGo AI:

  • unseengo_theme: Stores "dark" or "light" so your eyes aren't blinded by sudden page flips.
  • unseengo_city: Remembers your active selected city hub (e.g. "Kurnool" or "All India").
  • unseengo_user: Stores your encrypted session token if you are signed in.
  • unseengo_saved_places: Client-side cache of places you bookmark with the heart icon.

4. Disabling Cookies in Your Browser

You can block or purge cookies at any time via your browser settings. Note that disabling local storage may cause the website to default to dark mode and lose your saved offline itineraries upon refreshing.
