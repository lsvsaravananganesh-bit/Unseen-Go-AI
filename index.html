WHAT IS UNSEENGO AI?

Your smarter way to
explore India.

UnseenGo AI is a tourism discovery platform designed to take you beyond the usual tourist checklist and help you understand the places you visit.

01

Discover the unseen

Find lesser-known attractions alongside famous landmarks, so every city has something new to reveal.

02

Know the story

Learn the history, culture and background behind the places you discover instead of simply collecting locations.

03

Find your way

Open location information and map directions for individual places and explore them with confidence.

04

Plan the journey

Use accommodation information and the AI Planner to turn discoveries into a practical travel itinerary.
