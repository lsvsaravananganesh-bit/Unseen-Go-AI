✦ Recommended Gateway Airport

Hyderabad Intl (HYD) / Kurnool (KJB)

Fly into Hyderabad (HYD), then take a scenic 3.5-hour highway drive along NH44 to Kurnool. Regional turboprop flights also operate into Uyyalawada Narasimha Reddy Airport (KJB).

Primary Airport Code HYD / KJB

215 km (3.5 hr drive via NH44)

Flight Duration from Origin ~1 hr 15 mins non-stop

Daily flights from major metros

Onward Transport Recommendation Self-Drive / Pre-booked Cab

Pre-book intercity taxi at airport arrivals

Gateway Airports Directory for Off-Grid India

Quick reference for which flight hubs unlock specific cultural belts.

VDY / HBX

Jindal Vidyanagar & Hubli

Gateways to: Hampi, Anegundi, Badami

Vidyanagar Airport (VDY) is located just 35 km from Hampi with direct daily flights from Bengaluru and Hyderabad. Hubli (HBX) offers broader pan-India connections.

GAU / SHL

Guwahati & Shillong (Umroi)

Gateways to: Cherrapunji, Living Root Bridges, Kaziranga

Guwahati (GAU) has extensive high-frequency flights from across India. From Guwahati, smooth four-lane highways connect to Shillong in 3 hours, opening the doorway to Meghalaya's deep canyons.

Search Flights to GAU ↗
KUU / IXC

Bhuntar (Kullu) & Chandigarh

Gateways to: Spiti Valley, Lahaul & Tirthan

Kullu–Manali Airport at Bhuntar (KUU) handles regional Himalayan flights, while Chandigarh (IXC) is the primary base for travellers starting the high-altitude Shimla-Kinnaur-Kaza circuit.

Search Flights to IXC ↗
TIR / BLR

Tirupati & Bengaluru Intl

Gateways to: Lepakshi, Chandragiri, Talakona

Kempegowda Intl (BLR) is located right on the northern edge of Bengaluru, making the drive to Lepakshi just 100 km (2 hours via NH44). Tirupati Airport (TIR) unlocks the sacred hill shrines.
