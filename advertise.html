Partnership Formats

We decline generic programmatic banner ads and popups to preserve the tranquility of our user experience. Instead, we offer high-impact, contextual integration:

  • Signature Circuit Sponsorships: Co-present a curated driving trail (e.g. "The Tungabhadra Heritage Drive, presented by [Brand]").
  • Regional Tourism Board Spotlights: Dedicated state exploration showcases for state tourism departments (APTDC, KSTDC, RTDC, UP Tourism).
  • Verified Mobility & EV Hubs: Highlight EV fast-chargers or safe car rental stations along remote canyon and ghat routes.
  • Heritage Homestay Collections: Verified spotlighting for boutique eco-resorts and conservation lodges.
"All commercial integrations undergo rigorous vetting by our editorial board. We only feature brands that share our reverence for local ecology and cultural heritage."
