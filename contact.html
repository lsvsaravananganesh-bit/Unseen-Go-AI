✦ Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using UnseenGo AI.

How does UnseenGo AI discover hidden gems?
Our recommendation engine balances "hiddenness scores" against cultural depth, road connectivity, authentic verified photography, and seasonal climate factors. It identifies heritage monuments, canyons, and sacred sites with lower commercial tourist footfall but exceptional architectural and natural merit.
Is UnseenGo AI free to use for travelers?
Yes, all exploration, mapping, AI itinerary planning, and destination guides are completely free to browse. Travelers can also create a free account to save itineraries into their personal Travel Vault.
How do I report outdated road or temple information?
You can send a message via this Contact page or click the "Verify / Suggest Update" button on any place guide page. Our cultural curators verify every submission with local authorities and satellite imagery.
How can local homestays or heritage properties get listed?
Visit our List Your Hotel or Partner Portal page and submit your property details. We prioritize authentic, locally owned stays that reflect regional architecture.
