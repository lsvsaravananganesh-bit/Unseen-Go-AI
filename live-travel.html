Every live signal carries a provider/state. This prevents static or missing information from being presented as verified real-time data.

SignalSourceRule

UnseenGo does not invent live data. API providers may have coverage, quota, pricing and regional limitations. Event and transport availability is not the same as a confirmed booking.
