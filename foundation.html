How the Foundation Acts

Direct on-the-ground initiatives funded through platform proceeds and donations.

📜

Epigraphic & Stone Archival Digitization

We deploy LiDAR scanning and high-resolution photographic archival to document deteriorating Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, and Sanskrit stone inscriptions at neglected roadside shrines and stepwells.

🎨

Living Heritage Guild Grants

Direct micro-stipends provided to master stone carvers in Lepakshi, Kalamkari organic dye painters in Srikalahasti, and brass metal smiths across Andhra and Karnataka to preserve generational apprenticeships.

🌿

Clean Canyon & Cave Stewardship

We organize volunteer and village waste-retrieval expeditions in high-fragility gorges including Gandikota Canyon and Belum Cave surrounds, eradicating plastic debris from water catchments.

🎙️

Oral Folklore Preservation Studio

Recording elder community custodians, village priest lineages, and folk singers to ensure that local myths, sthala puranas, and sacred songs do not vanish with time.
